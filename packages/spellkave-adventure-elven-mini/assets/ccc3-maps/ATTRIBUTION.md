# Elven Tower map attribution

Map art by Derek Ruiz / Elven Tower Cartography, used under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode).
The filenames identify the variants. Spellkave resized/re-encoded the JPEGs
and adapted the layouts into adventure topology and spatial measurements.

Each JPEG was visually matched by normalised pixel comparison against the
same-named variant in the local CC collection staging cache. The creator's
separate April and July releases identify the collections covering 306 and 319.
