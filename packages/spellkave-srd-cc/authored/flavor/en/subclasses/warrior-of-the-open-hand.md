Open Hand warriors turn every breath, step, and empty palm into a weapon of disciplined grace. Their fighting style redirects charging foes, topples the overconfident, and leaves enemies reeling from a touch delivered at precisely the wrong moment. Beneath that calm motion lies a reservoir of inner strength, tempered to endure the violence they invite.
