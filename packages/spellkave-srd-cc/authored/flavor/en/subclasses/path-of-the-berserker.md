Berserkers surrender to the storm behind their ribs, becoming living engines of ruin when steel is drawn. Amid shattered shields and screaming battle lines, restraint falls away beneath a fierce, exultant hunger for the clash. Their fury is not subtle, but it is terrible: a force that breaks through fear, pain, and anything foolish enough to stand firm.
