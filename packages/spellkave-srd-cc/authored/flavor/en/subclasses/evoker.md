Evokers shape the raw violence of the elements into disciplined spectacle: fire that blooms like a siege engine, frost that stills a battlefield, thunder that shakes stone loose from towers. Their art can make a defender’s last stand possible or turn a distant ridge into a killing ground. Wherever danger gathers in ranks, an evoker’s spellcraft answers with ruinous light and sound.
