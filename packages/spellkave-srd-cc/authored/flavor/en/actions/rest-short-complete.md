You declare that you have finished at least 1 hour of qualifying downtime. This marks the end of your short rest. If you are hurt and have a Hit Point Die left, you spend one: roll it, add your Constitution modifier, and regain that many Hit Points. You have one die per level, and a Long Rest gives them all back.
