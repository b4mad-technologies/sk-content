Whispers from before the first crown and hearth linger behind the eyes, carrying fragments of crafts, instincts, and stubborn mortal virtues. Each secret arrives like an old scar remembered by the soul, leaving its bearer altered in small, useful ways. The elder powers rarely explain why they share such gifts.
