Bards turn story, rhythm, and daring into tools that can sway a crowded hall or steady companions in the thick of danger. They are wanderers, performers, and keen observers who collect secrets as readily as songs. In a crisis, a well-timed verse or cutting remark can change the course of an entire encounter.
