Paladins are sworn champions who turn conviction into action, standing where danger presses hardest. Whether serving a crown, a cause, or a private vow, they meet cruelty with disciplined courage and refuse to abandon those in their care. Their presence can rally wavering allies and make even hardened foes reconsider the cost of defiance.
