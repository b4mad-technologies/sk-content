Muscle under a groomed coat, scars pale along one shoulder, tack kept ready rather than pretty. The warhorse mouths the bit and stamps once, a sound with iron in it. When the reins come up, the fidgeting stops. The stable goes quiet when that stamp lands. Its tack is oiled, never ornamental.
