The pole is a long, straight shaft, smooth where hands have slid and rougher near the cut ends. It knocks once when set against the ground and rings faintly if tapped with a knuckle. Extended ahead, it becomes a cautious finger for corners and gaps the eyes have not yet agreed to trust.
