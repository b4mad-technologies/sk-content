The saddle is cut for a back that is not a horse's, the seat deep and the straps arranged in an unfamiliar pattern. Leather is supple, the stitching heavy, and a faint salt or high-wind smell clings to the pads. It looks incomplete until an unusual mount steps under it and the shape suddenly makes sense.
