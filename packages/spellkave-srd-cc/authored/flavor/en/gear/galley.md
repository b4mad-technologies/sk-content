Oars in long rows, a drum somewhere forward, and a deck that never quite dries. The galley smells of pitch, sweat, and salt. When the blades bite, the whole ship leans into the stroke like a single animal. The rowers' benches are polished by years of the same motion. Bilgewater finds every bare ankle.
