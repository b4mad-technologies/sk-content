The liquid is the color of weak tea and smells of dried herbs and a sickroom kettle. It leaves a thin film on the glass that does not rinse away at once. A sip is bitter, then merely dry. The vial looks ordinary until the light hits the residue. The residue smells stronger the next day than it did when poured.
