Weighted cord gathered into a loose coil, the knots biting the palm when it is lifted. It smells of tar and salt, and it wants to open. A practiced toss turns the tangle into a wide mouth. Until then it is only weight and the promise of a snare. Drying, it stiffens until the next throw loosens it.
