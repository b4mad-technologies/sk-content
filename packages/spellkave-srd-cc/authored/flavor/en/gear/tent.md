Canvas the color of wet sand, poles lashed alongside, the tent is a bundle until it is raised against the wind. The cloth smells of smoke and of damp mornings left out too long. Once pitched, the interior is a low, dim room that shuts the weather to a muffled hush, shelter enough for sleepers pressed close.
