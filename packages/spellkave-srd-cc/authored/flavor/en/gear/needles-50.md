A paper of fine needles, thin as grass stems, is wrapped so the points cannot find a finger by accident. They glint when the fold is opened, and they prick the cloth if the wrap is rushed. Blowgun ammunition, or a tailor's last resort, depending on the hand. The paper wrapper crackles like dry leaves.
