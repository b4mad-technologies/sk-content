A cleaner line than a plow beast, ears forward, coat brushed until it shines along the neck. The saddle smells of soap and old sweat. It shifts its weight when a rein lifts, already halfway into the next step. A good rider feels the next stride before the hoof lands, and the horse seems to know that.
