Broad through the chest, feathered at the hoof, the draft horse moves like a cart that learned patience. Harness leather has darkened its coat in stripes. It lowers a heavy head for a scratch and then leans, testing whether the handler can take the weight. Flies bother it less than a badly fitted collar.
