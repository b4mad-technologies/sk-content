The ground answers before the eye does: a slow, enormous footfall, then grey hide folded like old bark. The smell is warm animal and crushed grass. A howdah creaks somewhere above. Standing beside it, a person feels briefly the size of a rumor. The handler speaks softly, and the great ear turns to listen.
