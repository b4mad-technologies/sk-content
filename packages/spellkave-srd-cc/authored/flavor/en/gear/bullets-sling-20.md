Smooth stones, chosen for the same weight and the same sullen grey, rattle in a soft leather cup. A thumb finds the familiar egg-shape without looking. They smell of riverbank and pocket lint, and they leave a chalky dust in the seams of the pouch. One stone missing is a small, irritating absence.
