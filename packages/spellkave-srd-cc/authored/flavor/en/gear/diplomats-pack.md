The pack is neat where a traveler's bag is not: folded cloth that still holds a crease, a stoppered ink, paper that has not yet been stained. It smells of cedar and a little perfume. Opening it feels like arriving somewhere that expects manners. Strangers assume the owner has a letter worth reading.
