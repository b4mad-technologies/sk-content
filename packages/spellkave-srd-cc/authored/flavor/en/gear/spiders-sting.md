The vial is no bigger than a thumb, and the liquid inside is clear with a single dark thread that will not mix. It smells of dust and old webs. A drop on a blade looks like water until it dries to a faint gloss. The glass is cold. It would be easy to lose among ordinary sewing things, and hard to mistake once opened.
