A stiff leather tube, capped at both ends, keeps paper from the rain and from being folded into ruin. The caps seat with a hollow pop. Inside, sheets slide against one another with a dry whisper, safe from mud, sweat, and a careless elbow. A traveler learns the caps by feel, even in the dark.
