Halflings carry a talent for making room: at crowded tables, in wary company, and within homes brightened by small comforts. Their communities prize practical skill, quick laughter, and the stubborn belief that ordinary joys are worth protecting. Though often underestimated, they meet danger with nimble courage and a remarkable instinct for finding the hopeful path forward.
