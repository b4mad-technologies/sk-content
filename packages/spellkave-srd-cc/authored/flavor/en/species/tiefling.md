Tieflings carry a presence that makes ordinary rooms feel suddenly charged, whether through ember-bright eyes, curling horns, or a confidence sharpened by hard-won self-knowledge. Many learn early to read a crowd, answer suspicion with wit, and choose their own place in the world rather than wait for it to be offered. Their communities are as varied as their ambitions, bound more often by chosen loyalty than blood.
