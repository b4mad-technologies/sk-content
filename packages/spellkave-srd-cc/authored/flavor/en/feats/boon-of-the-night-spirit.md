In moonless halls and beneath starless boughs, the bearer becomes a rumor carried by cold air. Darkness gathers close like a familiar cloak, softening every edge and turning mortal substance strangely insubstantial. Only the keenest light, or a wound aimed at the mind, can readily find what the night has claimed.
