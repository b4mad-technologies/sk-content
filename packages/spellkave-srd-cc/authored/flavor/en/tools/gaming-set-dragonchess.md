Three stacked boards in a traveling box, pieces carved as tiny monsters and soldiers. They click into felt recesses. The set smells of cedar. Opening it in a tavern is a declaration that the evening will be long. Losing a piece in a tavern rush is a small tragedy. The lid closes only if every piece is home.
