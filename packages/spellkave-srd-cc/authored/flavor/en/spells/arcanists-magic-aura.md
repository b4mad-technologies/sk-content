A veil of practiced deceit settles over flesh or treasure, subtle as silk drawn across a lantern. Divinations find the story the caster has planted: a harmless trinket glimmers with forbidden promise, a potent relic falls quiet, or a living soul wears another nature beneath unseen scrutiny. With patient renewal, even suspicion can be taught to look away.
