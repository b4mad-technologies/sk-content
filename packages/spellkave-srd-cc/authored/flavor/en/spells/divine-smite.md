A weapon’s strike blooms with a sudden, sanctifying glare, as though dawn has been caught along its edge. The air rings with a clear, bell-like force, and shadows recoil from the wound it leaves behind. Against creatures steeped in death or malice, that radiance burns with an especially merciless purity.
