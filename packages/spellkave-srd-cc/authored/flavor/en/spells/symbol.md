A patient hand leaves a secret mark where stone, steel, or sealed wood can keep its silence. To careless eyes it is only a faint wrongness in the dust, but the chosen moment wakes it in cold radiance. Then the air itself remembers the inscription’s purpose: dread, agony, slumber, discord, stillness, or a final hollow touch.
