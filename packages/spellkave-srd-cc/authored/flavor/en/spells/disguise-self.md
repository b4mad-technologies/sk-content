A whisper of glamour slides over flesh, cloth, and carried steel, settling like moonlight on still water. Familiar features loosen into a chosen stranger’s smile, while colors, cuts, and trappings rearrange themselves to suit the masquerade. Only a searching hand finds the hollow places where the illusion has painted more than reality can hold.
