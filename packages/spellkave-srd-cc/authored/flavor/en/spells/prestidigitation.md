A flick of the wrist coaxes small wonders from the air: sparks that skitter like fireflies, a teasing breeze, the scent of rain on distant stone. Dust slips from a travel-stained cloak, embers wake or fade, and a cup of plain broth gains the warmth and savor of a remembered feast. It is the art of making the ordinary briefly behave as though it has a secret.
