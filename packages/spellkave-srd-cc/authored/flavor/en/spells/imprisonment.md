A hush falls over the condemned as the world chooses a shape for their absence: root-bound iron, dreamless sleep, a jewel’s cold brilliance, or a prison beyond familiar skies. Time passes without touching them, while seeking eyes and far-reaching omens find only silence. Their release waits behind a chosen deed, patient as prophecy and just as difficult to fulfill.
