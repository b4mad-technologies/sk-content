A borrowed ember stirs behind the teeth, tinting breath with the wild grandeur of ancient wyrms. When released, it erupts in a fierce wash of chosen ruin—searing flame, biting frost, crackling stormlight, caustic spray, or a choking venomous haze. For a few heartbeats, even an ordinary creature carries the terror of a dragon’s gaze.
