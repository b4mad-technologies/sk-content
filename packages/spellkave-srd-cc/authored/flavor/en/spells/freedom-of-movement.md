A quiet certainty settles over the chosen traveler, as though every road, current, and closing hand has forgotten how to hold them. Mud yields beneath their stride, water parts around their limbs, and binding cords find no purchase. Even in a crushing crowd or tangled wild, they move with the effortless grace of wind slipping through reeds.
