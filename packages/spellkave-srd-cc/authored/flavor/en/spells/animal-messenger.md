A crumb offered with gentle intent becomes a promise carried on quick paws or bright wings. The chosen creature slips through hedgerows, over roofs, and along moonlit trails, bearing a borrowed voice toward a familiar place. For a fleeting moment, the wild world remembers the shape of a human secret.
