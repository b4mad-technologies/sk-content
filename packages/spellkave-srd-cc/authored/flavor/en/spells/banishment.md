A sharp gesture draws an unseen boundary through the air, and the chosen figure seems suddenly out of place in the world. Sound dulls around it; colors flatten; then it is gone as though reality has closed a door. Some return with the startled breath of one pulled from a dream, while others are carried beyond the familiar sky to realms that remember their true nature.
