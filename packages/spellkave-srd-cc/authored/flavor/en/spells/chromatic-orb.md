A jewel-bright sphere gathers between the caster’s hands, its surface swirling with a chosen fury: biting rime, crackling stormlight, venomous vapor, roaring flame, corrosive gleam, or a concussive hum. It flies like a fragment of a violent sky, bursting against its mark in a flash of color and force. Sometimes the unleashed energy refuses to die, streaking onward in a hungry arc.
