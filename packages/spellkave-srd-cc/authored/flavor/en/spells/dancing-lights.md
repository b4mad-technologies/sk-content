A handful of pale flames bloom in the dark, drifting with the buoyant curiosity of fireflies that have forgotten the summer. They gather into a wavering procession, circle a weary traveler, or briefly suggest the silhouette of a silent companion. Their glow turns dust motes to stars and gives shadowed stone a softer face.
