A thought takes shape in the air: a watchful sentry, a barred gate, a prowling beast, or a curtain of impossible flame. It glides and gestures with convincing grace, painted in light but untouched by wind, scent, or sound. Only a careless hand—or a searching eye—finds the hollow space beneath its splendid surface.
