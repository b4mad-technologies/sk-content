A patient hand leaves a mark so faint it seems like a trick of dust and candlelight. Beneath that quiet sign sleeps a chosen catastrophe: a burst of elemental fury, a snare of borrowed magic, or a warning meant for one forbidden footfall. Doors, stones, and keepsakes become watchful things, keeping their silence until the wrong presence breaks it.
