A soft hush settles over wary fur and feather, carrying the comfort of a shared fire and an offered meal. Wild eyes lose their hard shine; ears tilt toward the caster’s voice, and even a creature born to flee may linger close. The bond is gentle as a hand held open, asking trust rather than demanding it.
