At the caster’s touch, flesh loosens into a pale drifting veil, garments and gear dissolving with it into the same silver vapor. The transformed traveler glides through cracks, keyholes, and crowded chambers as a breath of cold air, untouchable by most crude blows. Speech and steel alike fall silent until the mist gathers itself whole again.
