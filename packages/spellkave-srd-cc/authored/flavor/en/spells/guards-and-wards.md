Stone remembers every threshold pressed beneath the warder’s hand. Mist gathers in passageways, stairwells bloom with pale clinging threads, and familiar doors become stubborn secrets—or vanish into seamless masonry. At every turning, the halls seem to reconsider their own shape, while whispered warnings, prowling lights, foul vapors, or unseen winds keep unwelcome footsteps from finding a true path.
