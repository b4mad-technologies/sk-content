The world loses its color and weight, becoming a distant charcoal sketch beyond a veil of cold silver mist. Footsteps pass unheard through locked doors and crowded halls, while familiar faces drift by like memories seen through deep water. For a while, the traveler belongs to the hush between places, where walls are only suggestions and solitude has a shadow.
