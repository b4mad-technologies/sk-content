A wan gray lance slips through the air, carrying the chill of spent graves and winter-bare bones. Where it settles, muscle remembers fatigue: weapon arms tremble, claws lose their certainty, and every exertion feels like lifting against deep water. The victim’s strength does not vanish at once—it simply turns traitor beneath its skin.
