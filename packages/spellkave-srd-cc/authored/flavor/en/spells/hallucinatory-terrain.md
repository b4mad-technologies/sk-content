Across a chosen stretch of wilderness, the land dons another face: reeds whisper where dry earth lies, a harmless meadow seems to hide deep water, and stone paths vanish beneath imagined briars. Wind carries borrowed scents and sounds through the false landscape. Only a boot finding solid ground beneath a phantom marsh reveals the dream layered over the world.
