A colossal hand of pale force unfolds from empty air, its fingers flexing with the caster’s smallest motion. It can swat aside charging foes, close around a fleeing shape, or stand like a glittering barricade amid the chaos. Each gesture leaves a low, glassy tremor in the air, as though the world itself has been seized.
