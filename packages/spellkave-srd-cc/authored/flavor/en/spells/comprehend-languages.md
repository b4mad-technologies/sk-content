Soot-dark motes gather at the caster’s fingertips, then vanish like breath against a mirror. Foreign speech unfolds into plain intent, while inked marks beneath a resting hand seem to stir with the remembered voices of those who made them. Yet riddles keep their teeth, and secrets hidden behind ciphers remain silent.
