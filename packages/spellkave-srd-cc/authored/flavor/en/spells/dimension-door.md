Space folds like silk drawn through a ring, and the caster vanishes between one heartbeat and the next. A companion may be caught in that impossible shortcut, carried through a breathless dark where direction becomes a matter of thought. Then stone, starlight, or open air rushes back into being exactly where intent has led.
