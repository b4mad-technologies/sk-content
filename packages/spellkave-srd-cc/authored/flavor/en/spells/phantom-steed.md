Mist gathers at the caster’s feet, knitting itself into the proud silhouette of a horse shaped from moonlit vapor and half-remembered starlight. Its tack gleams with borrowed substance, its hooves striking no true earth as it races beneath its rider. At journey’s end, the apparition thins into drifting smoke, leaving only silence in its wake.
