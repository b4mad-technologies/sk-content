With a grinding roar, the earth answers a shaping hand, thrusting up a barricade of fresh-hewn rock. Dust rolls through the air as impossible arches, ramps, and rugged parapets knit themselves to cliff and ruin alike. For a brief, breathless while, the battlefield belongs to whoever can command the stone.
