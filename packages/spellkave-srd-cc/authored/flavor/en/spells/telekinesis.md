An unseen grasp closes across the air, answering thought with terrible precision. Stones drift like dust, iron hinges groan open, and armed foes may find the ground abandoning them beneath a silent, immovable will. In deft hands, the same force can turn a key, uncork a vial, or pluck a prized possession from trembling fingers.
