In lightless halls, shadows separate into shades of charcoal and silver rather than swallowing the way ahead. A lifetime of twilight sharpens every outline: damp stone, a lurking silhouette, the faint glint of steel beneath a cloak. Darkness becomes a veil, not a wall.
