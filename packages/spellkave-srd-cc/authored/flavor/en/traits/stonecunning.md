With palm against a cold wall or boots planted on ancient flagstones, the world begins to murmur. Faint shivers travel through bedrock, carrying the weight of footsteps, settling mortar, and hidden movement beyond sight. In caverns and ruined halls, stone becomes a patient, whispering guide.
