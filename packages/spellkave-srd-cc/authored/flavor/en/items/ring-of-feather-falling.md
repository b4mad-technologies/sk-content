A pale silver band, light as shed down, seems to resist the pull of every outstretched hand. When its bearer slips from a ledge or vanishes into open air, the world’s roar softens to a hush and the long drop becomes a drifting descent. Tiny motes of light trail behind it like feathers caught in moonlit wind.
