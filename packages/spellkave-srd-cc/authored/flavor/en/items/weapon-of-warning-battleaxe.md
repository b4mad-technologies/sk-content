Its broad steel head gives a faint, restless hum whenever danger gathers nearby, like a storm trapped beneath the forge-dark metal. In camp, the axe lies cold and still until the first hostile breath breaks the night, then shudders awake with a warning that races through every sleeping companion. Those who carry its echo into battle find themselves moving before fear has time to speak.
