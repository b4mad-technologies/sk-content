Cool marble curls into the likeness of a trumpeting elephant, its raised trunk seeming to hold a breath of distant thunder. In the palm, it is smooth and solemn; cast upon open ground, it answers with the immense presence of a patient, tusked companion. When its borrowed life ebbs, the stone remains faintly warm beneath the fingers.
