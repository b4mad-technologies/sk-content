The helm’s old bronze brow-band is chased with tiny scripts that seem to rearrange whenever unfamiliar voices rise nearby. Beneath its cool weight, foreign speech loses its thorns and becomes a flowing current of meaning. Even the scratch of a distant quill or the cadence of a market cry may seem to carry a hidden, patient clarity.
