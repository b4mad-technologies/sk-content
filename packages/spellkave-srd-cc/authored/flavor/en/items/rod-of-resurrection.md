The rod is dark wood capped in dull metal, and it holds a warmth like a wrist pressed to a sleeping chest. Held near a still face, the warmth gathers rather than spreads. The air around the tip smells of clean linen and a window opened at dawn. Something in the wood seems to be listening for a breath that has not come back yet.
