A pale, thirsty powder that clings to the fingertips like flour from an abandoned mill. Scattered across a pool, it draws the water inward with a soft, impossible hush, leaving behind a tiny bead that trembles with captive tide. Even creatures born of surf and rain recoil from its dry, grave-cold touch.
