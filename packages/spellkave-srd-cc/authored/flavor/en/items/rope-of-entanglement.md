A coil of pale braided cord lies quiet as a sleeping serpent, its fibers cool and faintly alive beneath the hand. At a whispered impulse it unspools with startling purpose, lashing around fleeing limbs and drawing tight in a patient, unyielding embrace. When the struggle ends, it gathers itself again in neat, innocent loops.
