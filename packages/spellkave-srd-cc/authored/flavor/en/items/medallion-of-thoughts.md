A cool silver disc that seems to drink in stray whispers from crowded rooms. Against the breastbone, it hums with the uneasy intimacy of overheard secrets, its polished face clouding briefly when nearby minds stir with strong feeling. At dawn, a faint warmth returns to its chain, as though the night has taught it new thoughts.
