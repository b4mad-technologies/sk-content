Its dark barrel bears a mirrored sheen, catching threats at the edge of sight before they draw near. In a steady hand, the pistol seems to weigh each heartbeat, lending its certainty either to the shot or to the space between steel and skin. A faint, cool pressure gathers around its wielder when danger closes.
