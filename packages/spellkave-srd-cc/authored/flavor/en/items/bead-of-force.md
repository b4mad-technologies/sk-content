A bead of force is a midnight-black pearl that seems to drink the light around it, cold and unnaturally dense between finger and thumb. When cast, it answers with a silent flash and the sudden certainty of an invisible wall closing around the world. Within its glass-clear prison, even a furious struggle becomes a drifting, muffled spectacle.
