Three dark-mouthed sleeves nest along the quiver’s side, each deeper than its stitched silhouette permits. A hand reaches in past cool leather and finds feather, shaft, or polished wood waiting exactly where instinct expects. Though packed for a campaign’s worth of troubles, it rides with the light sway of an empty hunter’s kit.
