A milky stone keeps a close orbit, a thin brightness that refuses to be called a halo. The air just outside the shoulders feels a little thicker. A raised arm finds the circle still there, untroubled. It is cool and smells of clean steel. Even a careless elbow seems to meet the brightness before it meets a wall.
