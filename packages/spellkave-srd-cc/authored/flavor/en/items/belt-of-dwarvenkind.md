The belt is thick hide stitched over a band of cold metal, and it settles on the hips like a lintel. A mineral smell rises from the buckle, wet stone after a hammer strike. Standing in it, the knees feel planted and the shoulders drop. Hair at the jaw, if any grows there, seems to remember a deeper color.
