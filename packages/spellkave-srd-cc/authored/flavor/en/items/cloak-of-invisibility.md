The cloak is a full length of dull cloth, too broad to fold into a pocket, and light slides off it as if the weave were oiled. Held up, the room behind it looks thinner. A draft moves the hem while the shoulders underneath feel briefly unaccounted for. Eyes keep trying to finish a shape the cloth refuses to hold.
