A clay-brown bead sits warm in a closed fist, lighter than bread and heavier than a promise. When it breaks, the air fills with the smell of a just-cut loaf and a kettle left on the coals. Hunger loosens its grip before the last crumb is gone. The mouth remembers a meal that never touched a plate.
