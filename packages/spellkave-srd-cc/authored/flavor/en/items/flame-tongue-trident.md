At a murmured command, ember-red fire races along the trident’s three prongs without consuming shaft, grip, or bearer. Its blazing crown paints wet stone and armored faces in restless bronze, while the air around each thrust shivers with furnace heat. Even at rest, the weapon carries the faint scent of smoke after rain.
