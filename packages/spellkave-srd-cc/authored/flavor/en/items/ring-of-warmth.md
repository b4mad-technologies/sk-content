A band of dull silver that keeps a quiet ember against the skin, even beneath wind-scoured mail. Frost gathering on a traveler’s cloak softens to glittering dew, and bitter air loses its teeth around the hand that bears it. In deep winter, the ring’s warmth feels less like fire than a remembered hearth.
