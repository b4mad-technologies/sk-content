Pale crystal lenses settle over the eyes with a cool, silken weight, tinting every face with a hint of promise. In candlelight, their facets catch soft colors that seem to linger around a smile or spoken kindness. Those who meet their wearer’s gaze may feel, for a fleeting moment, as though an old and welcome acquaintance has just entered the room.
