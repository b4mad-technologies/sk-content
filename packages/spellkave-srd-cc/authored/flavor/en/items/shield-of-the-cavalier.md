The shield carries the smell of saddle leather and horse sweat even when no mount is near, and its weight wants a rhythm of hooves. It is a full arm's cover, awkward in a doorway and sure once the shoulder is set. A grip on the strap makes the stance widen. Dust on the rim looks recently kicked up.
