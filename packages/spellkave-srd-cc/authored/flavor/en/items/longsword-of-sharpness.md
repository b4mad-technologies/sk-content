Its edge catches the light in a line so fine it seems to divide the world rather than reflect it. Stone, timber, and iron yield beneath its passing with an unnerving clean finality, while a telling stroke leaves even hardiest foes pale and wavering. The hilt remains cool and perfectly balanced, as though the blade hungers only for the exact moment of contact.
