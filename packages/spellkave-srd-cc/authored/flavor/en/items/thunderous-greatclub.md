The greatclub is a length of dark, knotted wood that makes the air feel thicker before it moves. It will not hide behind a leg, let alone in a hand. A practice swing leaves a low pressure in the ears, like a door slammed in a stone hall. The grip smells of pitch and a storm that stayed indoors.
