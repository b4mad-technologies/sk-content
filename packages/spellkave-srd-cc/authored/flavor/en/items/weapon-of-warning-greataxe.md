Its broad, moon-pale blade hums faintly in the hush before danger, as though listening through the ground for marching feet. Resting beside a sleeper, the greataxe grows cold at the first stir of violence and sends a sharp shiver through nearby dreams. In battle, its haft seems to tug toward the instant before the first blow falls.
