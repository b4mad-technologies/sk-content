A cedar box of jewel-bright paints waits with the patient hush of a sealed secret. Each stroke leaves a faint scent of rain, stone dust, or crushed petals, and the painted world seems to hold its breath until the final line is drawn. Doors, thorns, and shadowed hollows may answer the brush—but greed and wild flame make poor subjects for such delicate color.
