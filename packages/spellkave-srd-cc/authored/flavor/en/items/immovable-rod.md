A plain iron bar with a thumb-worn stud at one end, it carries a peculiar stillness even before it is set. When awakened, the air around it seems to tighten, as though the world has found a single point it cannot argue with. Suspended over a chasm or braced beneath a gate, it waits in perfect, stubborn silence.
