Four pale shoes, light as shed leaves, cling to a mount’s hooves with a whisper of wind. Once fitted, they carry horse and rider just above the world: over reeds without stirring them, across snow without a print, and through treacherous ground as though it were a smooth road. Even after a long day beneath the open sky, the steed’s breath remains cool and steady.
