The shield is broad enough to cover a torso, and its face has a dull sheen that seems to watch the edge of the room. It is not a thing that fits in a palm. A strap creaks when the arm settles in, and the eyes want to check a corner that was empty a moment ago. The metal smells of oil and night air.
