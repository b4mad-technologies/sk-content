The scroll is a narrow roll of stiff parchment, small enough to tuck into a sleeve and dense enough to matter. Ink on the inner face looks wet if the light is wrong, though the fingers come away dry. Unrolling it fills the mouth with the taste of a word not yet spoken. Once the eye has finished the line, the page seems eager to be blank.
