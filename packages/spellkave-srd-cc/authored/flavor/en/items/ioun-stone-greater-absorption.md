This larger grey stone rides a slower circle and pulls a wider dimness out of the air around the head. Sparks that pass near it lose their bite before they fade. The orbit has weight. After a while the crystal looks like a well that has not yet filled. The circle it keeps never quite lets a spark finish its trip.
