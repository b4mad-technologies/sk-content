A polished dart that seems to hover a heartbeat longer than it should before the throw. Its balance shifts with the wielder’s intent: eager and needle-true when danger must be met, then suddenly steadfast, as though an unseen shield has closed around the hand that holds it. In torchlight, its narrow point catches a cold, watchful gleam.
