A lacquered case of thin, cool cards, each painted with figures that seem to shift whenever the eye wanders. One cast upon the earth may unfurl into a startling presence: a beast, a warrior, or something grand enough to steal the breath from a silent room. When the apparition fades, its card is left pale and empty, like a dream spent at dawn.
