A homunculus is a palm-sized servant shaped by careful hands and stubborn purpose, its bright eyes reflecting the will of its maker. It flits through workshops, hidden passages, and shadowed halls with unnerving patience. Though fragile in appearance, it guards its assigned secrets with the fierce devotion of something that has never known another life.
