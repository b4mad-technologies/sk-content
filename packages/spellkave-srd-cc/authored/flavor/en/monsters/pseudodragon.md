A pseudodragon is a sly, cat-sized drake with bright eyes and a habit of appearing where secrets gather. It slips through rafters and thornbushes alike, watching strangers with unnerving patience before deciding whether to play, flee, or strike. Those who earn its trust gain a fiercely possessive companion with a taste for mischief.
