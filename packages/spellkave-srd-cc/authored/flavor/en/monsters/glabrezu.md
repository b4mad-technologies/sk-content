A glabrezu arrives beneath a scent of hot iron and extinguished candles, its many claws closing around bargains no mortal remembers making. It speaks with unsettling patience, offering secrets, wealth, or revenge in exchange for a promise that seems harmless at first. When deception fails, its towering form turns the chamber into a place of snapping pincers, broken stone, and panic.
