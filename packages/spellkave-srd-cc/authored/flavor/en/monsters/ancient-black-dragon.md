An ancient black dragon rules its mire-shrouded domain with a patience more dreadful than rage. Its presence sours the air, blackens still water, and turns every reed bed and ruin into a possible ambush. Those who trespass often glimpse only a vast silhouette and hear the slow hiss of laughter before the swamp closes over their trail.
