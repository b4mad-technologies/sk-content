Frost giants stride from wind-scoured peaks and glacier caverns with the certainty of avalanches. Their halls are loud with feasting, boasts, and the ringing of iron, but strangers are met with cold suspicion and crushing force. When roused to battle, a frost giant turns the frozen wilderness itself into a weapon, hurling ice and scattering lesser foes beneath enormous steps.
