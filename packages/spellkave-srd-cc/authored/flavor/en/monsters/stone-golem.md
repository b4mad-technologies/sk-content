A stone golem stands in perfect stillness until its charge is threatened, then moves with the awful certainty of a collapsing wall. Its carved features never change, but every footfall shakes dust from old ceilings and cracks flagstones beneath its weight. Patient, tireless, and utterly obedient, it is a guardian built to outlast generations of intruders.
