A roper waits in perfect stillness among cavern stone, its knotted hide and mineral-crusted limbs easily mistaken for part of the wall. When prey strays near, pale tendrils uncoil from the darkness and drag it toward a toothy maw beneath the creature’s rocky bulk. Deep caves often fall silent around a roper’s hunting ground, save for the scrape of something being pulled across stone.
