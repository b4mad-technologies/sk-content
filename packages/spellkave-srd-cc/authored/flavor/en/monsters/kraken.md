A kraken is less a beast than a calamity rising from the deep, its vast limbs turning calm waters into a choking maze of foam and wreckage. Sailors dread the silence that falls before it surfaces, when even the boldest crew feels the sea listening. Those who glimpse its ancient, unblinking gaze rarely forget the terror of being noticed.
