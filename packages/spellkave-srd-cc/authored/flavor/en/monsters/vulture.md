Vultures wheel high above lonely roads, battlefields, and sun-blasted wastes, patient as omens. Their hooked beaks and ragged wings make them unwelcome camp companions, yet they are rarely hunters of the healthy. When a flock settles nearby, even hardened travelers grow quiet, watching the horizon for whatever drew the birds down.
