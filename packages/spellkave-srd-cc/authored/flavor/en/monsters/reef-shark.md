Reef sharks glide through warm shallows with tireless, watchful purpose, their pale shapes vanishing beneath sun-dappled waves. Most keep to the edge of reefs and sandbars, but blood in the water or frantic splashing can draw them into a sudden, circling frenzy. Sailors learn to respect the calm water where their fins break the surface.
