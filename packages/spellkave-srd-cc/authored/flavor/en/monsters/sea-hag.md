A sea hag waits where black water meets broken stone, her laughter threading through fog like a distant gull’s cry. Sailors speak of faces glimpsed beneath the waves and dreams that sour into dread before dawn. She favors loneliness, despair, and the slow certainty that no shore is close enough to save the lost.
