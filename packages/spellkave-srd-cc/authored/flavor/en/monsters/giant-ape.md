A giant ape rules its territory with thunderous displays and startling intelligence. It watches intruders from the high canopy or crumbling ruins, weighing whether they are a threat before crashing into action. When roused, its fury turns trees, stones, and shattered walls into weapons, but a calm offering or retreat may avert its wrath.
