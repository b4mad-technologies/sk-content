A zombie lurches from silence with the stubborn purpose of a body that has forgotten how to die. Its ruined flesh carries the stink of damp earth and old blood, while vacant eyes fix on any living movement nearby. Alone it is a grim nuisance; in a shambling mass, it becomes an inexorable tide of grasping hands.
