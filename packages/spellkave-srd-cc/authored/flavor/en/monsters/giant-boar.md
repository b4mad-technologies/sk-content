A giant boar is a battering ram of bristled muscle, quick to turn a quiet woodland path into a churned battlefield. It defends feeding grounds and young with startling fury, lowering its tusks before crashing through brush, fences, and anything foolish enough to stand firm. Even after the charge, its furious squeals carry far through the trees.
