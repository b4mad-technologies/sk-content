At first glance, a gargoyle is only weathered stone perched above a gate or ruined hall. Then dust sifts from its joints, claws scrape the masonry, and its carved snarl becomes terribly alive. Patient and territorial, it waits for trespassers to pass beneath its chosen perch before descending in a blur of wings, teeth, and shattered stone.
