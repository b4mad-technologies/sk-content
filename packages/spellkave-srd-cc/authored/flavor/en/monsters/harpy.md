A harpy’s song drifts through lonely cliffs and ruined towers, sweet enough to make danger feel like a distant dream. Beneath the melody waits a cruel, carrion-hearted predator that delights in leading the enchanted into peril. Its nest is often marked by scattered treasures and the silence of those who followed the music too far.
