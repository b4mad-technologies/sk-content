A doppelganger moves through crowded streets and lonely halls wearing a borrowed face, studying every gesture before the deception is tested. Its imitation is unsettlingly close, yet small absences of warmth or memory can betray the thing beneath. When cornered, the stolen identity melts away, revealing a pale predator built for sudden violence and escape.
