A darkmantle clings motionless to cavern ceilings, resembling a damp fold of stone or fungus until prey passes below. It drops in sudden silence, smothering light and wrapping its rubbery body around a victim’s head. In the dark that follows, the scrape of many other shapes against the rock can be far more frightening than the first attack.
