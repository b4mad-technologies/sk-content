A kobold warrior is rarely found alone; its shrill calls and skittering footsteps quickly draw allies from tunnels, ruins, and shadowed burrows. Small hands grip scavenged weapons with surprising discipline, especially when a larger foe strays into a prepared ambush. Though easily rattled when isolated, a pack of kobolds can turn cramped ground into a frantic, dangerous battlefield.
