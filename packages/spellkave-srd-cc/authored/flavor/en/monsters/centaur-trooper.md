Centaur troopers thunder across open ground in disciplined ranks, carrying the momentum of cavalry without need of saddle or mount. Their camps are orderly, their patrols tireless, and their war cries roll over the hills before their lances do. An enemy who mistakes them for wild raiders soon learns how swiftly woodland pride becomes battlefield precision.
