An air elemental arrives as a sudden, purposeful gale, scattering dust, snuffing lamps, and turning loose cloth into snapping banners. Its shape is only briefly visible in whorls of vapor and airborne debris before it breaks apart and reforms elsewhere. In enclosed spaces, its presence makes every breath feel borrowed and every open doorway a threat.
