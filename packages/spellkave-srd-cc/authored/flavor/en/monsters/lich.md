A lich is the cold persistence of a tyrant, scholar, or warlord who refused to let death close its book. Draped in the remnants of mortal grandeur, it haunts sealed crypts and ruined halls, guarding secrets that have outlived kingdoms. Its presence turns every shadow watchful and every promise of treasure into a warning.
