Lean, wary, and endlessly opportunistic, jackals drift along lonely roads and the edges of camps in search of an easy meal. Their sharp yips carry farther than their small frames suggest, often answered by hidden packmates in the scrub. Though quick to flee from a stronger foe, they can become bold when hunger or numbers favor them.
