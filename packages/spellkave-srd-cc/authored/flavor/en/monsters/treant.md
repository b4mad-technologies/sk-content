A treant moves with the patience of deep roots and the force of a falling oak. Mossy bark conceals a keen guardian’s presence until branches bend into grasping limbs and the forest itself seems to take offense. Those who honor the wild may find an ancient ally; those who bring axe or flame awaken its terrible wrath.
