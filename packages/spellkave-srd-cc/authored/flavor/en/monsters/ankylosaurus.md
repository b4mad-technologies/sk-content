Low and broad beneath a mantle of knobby armor, the ankylosaurus moves with the patience of a living fortress. Its heavy tail ends in a bone-shattering club, swung only when warning snorts and stamping feet fail to drive trouble away. Though herbivorous, it fiercely guards its grazing grounds and young from anything foolish enough to press too close.
