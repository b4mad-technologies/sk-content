Grimlocks haunt lightless tunnels where every scrape of stone and shift of breath carries meaning. Blind faces and pale skin lend them a corpse-like stillness, but they move with unnerving certainty through their chosen caves. Intruders often notice the silence first, followed by the sudden realization that something below has been listening all along.
