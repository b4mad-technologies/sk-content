An archmage bends the hidden laws of magic with the calm precision of a master artisan, turning a battlefield into a shifting puzzle of fire, frost, shadow, and force. Such spellcasters are often surrounded by wards, servants, and strange relics gathered over long years of dangerous study. Facing one means contending not only with a powerful foe, but with a mind that has prepared for nearly every possibility.
