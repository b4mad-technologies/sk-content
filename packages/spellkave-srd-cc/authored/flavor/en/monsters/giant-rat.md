A giant rat slips through ruined cellars and shadowed alleys with a twitching nose and tireless hunger. Its yellowed teeth can worry through wood, leather, and careless supplies, while its keen senses make hiding from it difficult. Alone it is a nasty scavenger; in numbers, it becomes a crawling tide of panic and filth.
