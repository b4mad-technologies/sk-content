A brown bear is a mountain of fur, muscle, and blunt patience until something threatens its territory, cubs, or meal. Its heavy footfalls and low, rumbling warnings often reach the forest trail before its broad shape appears between the trees. Even a wounded bear remains dangerously determined, fighting with the furious force of a creature that refuses to be driven off.
