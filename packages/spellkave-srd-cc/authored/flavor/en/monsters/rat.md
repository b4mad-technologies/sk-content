A rat is a quick, watchful scavenger that thrives wherever crumbs, refuse, and shadows gather. It slips through cracks too small for most predators, vanishing beneath floorboards or into tangled roots at the first hint of danger. Though easily startled alone, a cluster of rats can turn a quiet cellar into a rustling, red-eyed nuisance.
