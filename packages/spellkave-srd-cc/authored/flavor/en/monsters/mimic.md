A mimic turns ordinary surroundings into a trap, wearing the shape of a chest, door, table, or any other tempting piece of dungeon clutter. Its stillness is unnerving only in retrospect, when wood sprouts teeth and metal hinges become grasping limbs. Survivors learn to distrust anything that seems too conveniently placed.
