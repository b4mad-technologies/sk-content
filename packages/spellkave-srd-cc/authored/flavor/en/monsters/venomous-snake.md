A venomous snake lies coiled in sun-warmed cracks, leaf litter, or the shadowed corners of old stone. It strikes with startling speed when threatened or cornered, then slips away to let its bite do the work. Most avoid larger creatures, but a disturbed nest or narrow path can turn a quiet encounter into a desperate one.
