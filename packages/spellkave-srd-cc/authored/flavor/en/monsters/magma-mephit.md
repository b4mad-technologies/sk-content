A magma mephit scuttles from cracks in volcanic stone, its jagged body glowing through seams of cooling crust. It delights in smoke, heat, and sudden bursts of molten spite, often serving stronger fire-dwellers for the chance to spread ruin. Its rasping laughter is usually the last warning before the ground itself spits flame.
