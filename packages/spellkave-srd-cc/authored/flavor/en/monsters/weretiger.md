A weretiger moves through civilized streets with a predator’s patience, wearing a familiar face until moonlight or fury strips the disguise away. In beast form, it is all silent muscle, bright eyes, and sudden violence; in human form, its pride and territorial instincts are harder to hide. Those who earn its respect may find a fierce guardian, while trespassers become quarry.
