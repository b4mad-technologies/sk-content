A panther moves through the undergrowth like a patch of midnight given claws, its presence betrayed only by the sudden silence of smaller beasts. Patient and fiercely territorial, it favors ambush over pursuit, striking from shadowed rocks or low branches. Those who cross its hunting ground may never glimpse more than green eyes and a flick of black fur.
