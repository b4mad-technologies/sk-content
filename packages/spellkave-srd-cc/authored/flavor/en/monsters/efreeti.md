An efreeti arrives in a gust of furnace heat, regal and terrible amid the scent of scorched metal. It treats mortal bargains as chains to be tightened, rewarding obedience with splendor and punishing insult with spectacular cruelty. Where it lingers, stone glows warm beneath the feet and even the shadows seem to smolder.
