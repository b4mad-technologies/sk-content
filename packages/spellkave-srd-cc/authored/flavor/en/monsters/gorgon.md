A gorgon is a hulking, iron-plated beast whose breath rolls across the ground in a choking haze. Its hooves strike sparks from stone as it charges, scattering lesser creatures before its heavy horns. Hunters who mistake it for a mere bull often discover too late that even a glancing encounter can leave flesh stiff and lifeless as carved stone.
