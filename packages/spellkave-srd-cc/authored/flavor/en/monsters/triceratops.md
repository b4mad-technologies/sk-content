A living battering ram crowned with three wicked horns, the triceratops moves through fern-choked valleys with stubborn, thunderous purpose. Its broad frill flares as it guards a nest, a watering place, or the safety of its herd. Even seasoned hunters give it room when the ground begins to tremble beneath its charge.
