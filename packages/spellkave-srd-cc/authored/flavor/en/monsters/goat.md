A goat picks its way across crumbling ledges and thorny hillsides with infuriating confidence, treating impossible footing as a private joke. Its sharp eyes miss little, especially unattended packs, garden fences, and anything that might be edible. Though usually stubborn rather than hostile, a cornered goat can turn a peaceful path into a sudden, horn-led commotion.
