A flesh golem lurches from the shadows as a patchwork nightmare of stitched muscle, pallid skin, and crude metal fittings. Its maker’s purpose drives it with tireless, literal determination, whether standing guard in a silent vault or hunting an assigned quarry through rain and ruin. Lightning can jolt its dead tissues into sudden, terrible motion.
