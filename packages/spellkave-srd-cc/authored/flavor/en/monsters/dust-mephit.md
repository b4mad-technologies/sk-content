A dust mephit drifts from crumbling walls and wind-scoured ruins with a rasping laugh and grit in its wake. It delights in turning clear air into choking haze, darting through the confusion to nip at exposed ankles. Even when driven off, its lingering dust seems to whisper from every cracked stone.
