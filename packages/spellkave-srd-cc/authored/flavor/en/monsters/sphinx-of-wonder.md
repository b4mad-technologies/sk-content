A sphinx of wonder drifts through ancient halls on silent wings, its eyes bright with impossible colors and sudden inspiration. Rather than devouring trespassers, it delights in testing their curiosity with riddles that reshape the room, summon fleeting visions, or turn certainty into laughter. Those who answer with imagination may leave carrying a secret no map can show.
