Baboons move through rocky gullies and thorny scrub in noisy, watchful bands, their sharp barks carrying far beyond the trees. Though quick to flee a larger threat, they can turn fierce when cornered or when defending food, young, or a favored sleeping ledge. A troop’s sudden silence is often the first warning that something dangerous is nearby.
