A giant toad lurks where stagnant pools meet tangled reeds, its mottled hide nearly indistinguishable from mud and moss. It waits with patient, amphibian stillness until prey strays close, then erupts from the water in a startling blur of jaws and tongue. Even after the splash fades, its low, throaty croak can unsettle a marsh camp.
