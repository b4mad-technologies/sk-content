An owlbear is a hulking tangle of feathers, fur, hooked beak, and tearing claws, its bellow carrying through dark woods like a warning horn. Fiercely territorial and quick to rage, it charges intruders with startling speed before pinning them beneath its immense weight. Travelers who hear branches snapping overhead often discover too late that the forest is not empty.
