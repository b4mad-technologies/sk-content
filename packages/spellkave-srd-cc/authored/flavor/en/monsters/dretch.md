A dretch is a squat, wheezing fiend whose sour stench arrives before its claws do. It scuttles through darkness in snarling packs, emboldened by cruelty and the misery of larger demons. Though cowardly when cornered alone, it becomes vicious amid confusion, eager to drag the weak into filth and panic.
