A wererat thrives where crowded streets, cellar tunnels, and forgotten drains offer endless places to vanish. In mortal guise, it cultivates suspicion with whispered bargains and stolen secrets; when cornered, its true nature erupts in claws, fangs, and frantic cunning. Few infestations begin with a scream—most begin with something missing.
