Merfolk skirmishers strike from the shifting blue, using reefs, kelp forests, and sudden currents as cover. Their bright signals and sharp calls carry through the water before a coordinated rush of spears and nets breaks the surface calm. Those who threaten their waters often find the shore itself has become hostile.
