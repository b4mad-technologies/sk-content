A pale lantern-glow bobs through marsh mist and ruined halls, always just beyond easy reach. It flickers with an uncanny suggestion of purpose, drawing the lost toward deeper water, broken ground, or forgotten doors. When cornered, the little light turns sharp and spiteful, crackling with sudden malice before vanishing into the dark.
