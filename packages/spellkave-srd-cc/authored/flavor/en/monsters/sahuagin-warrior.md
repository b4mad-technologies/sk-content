Sahuagin warriors rise from black water with cruel discipline, their serrated weapons flashing before the surf can settle. They hunt as a tide-born pack, driving prey toward reefs, wreckage, and deeper currents where panic does their work. Even on shore, they carry the cold patience of the sea and the certainty that every shoreline is only borrowed land.
