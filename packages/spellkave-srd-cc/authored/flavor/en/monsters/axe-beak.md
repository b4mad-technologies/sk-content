Axe beaks are towering, flightless hunters whose hooked bills can split bark, bone, and shieldwood with equal ease. Their booming calls carry across open plains, often answered by the thunder of many clawed feet. A startled herd can turn a quiet trail into a stampede of snapping beaks and flying dust.
