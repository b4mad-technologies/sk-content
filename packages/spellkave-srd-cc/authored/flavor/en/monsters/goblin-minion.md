Small, quick, and loud when courage fails, goblin minions thrive in the confusion around stronger foes. They dart from cover to cover, hurling insults as readily as weapons, then scatter the moment a fight turns against them. Alone they are skittish nuisances; in a pack, their reckless energy can turn a quiet ruin into a frantic ambush.
