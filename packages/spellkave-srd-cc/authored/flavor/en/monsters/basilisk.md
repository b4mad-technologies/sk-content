A basilisk moves through ruined cellars and sunless gullies with patient, reptilian certainty, leaving scavengers to trail its path at a wary distance. Its stare carries a dreadful stillness that turns bold intruders into silent monuments. Clever hunters avoid its gaze, but even reflected glimpses can make a lair feel like a hall of cursed statues.
