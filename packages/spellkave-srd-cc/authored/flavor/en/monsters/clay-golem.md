A clay golem lumbers from its post with the grim purpose of a sculptor’s curse given legs. Its earthen body bears old cracks, ritual sigils, and the stains of whatever sacred hall or sealed vault it was made to guard. It does not tire, bargain, or flee; intruders meet only the slow certainty of an ancient command.
