A drider moves through ruined halls and moonless caverns with the dreadful patience of a spider that remembers being something else. Its web-choked lairs are full of false paths, dangling bones, and silken snares laid for the curious. When it finally emerges from the dark, the encounter feels less like a hunt than a judgment descending on trembling legs.
