An oni moves through the mortal world behind a borrowed face, savoring fear as much as flesh. Its voice can turn honeyed or thunderous in an instant, luring travelers from lantern-lit roads into fog-choked woods. When deception fails, the disguise falls away to reveal a towering hunter wrapped in storm clouds and old hunger.
