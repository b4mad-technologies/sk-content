An azer sentinel stands motionless amid forge-smoke and ringing iron, its brass-bound frame glowing from within like a banked furnace. It guards sacred workshops, sealed vaults, and masterwork halls with tireless attention. When intruders cross its charge, the air thickens with heat and the sentinel’s hammer falls with the certainty of a closing gate.
