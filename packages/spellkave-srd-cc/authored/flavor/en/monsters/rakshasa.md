A rakshasa moves through mortal society wrapped in silk, courtesy, and the faint perfume of danger. Its charm is a mask worn for years if needed, concealing a predator that feeds on bargains, grudges, and carefully cultivated ruin. When cornered, the elegant façade falls away, revealing a fiendish hunter with unsettling, backward-facing hands.
