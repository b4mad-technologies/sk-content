Gnoll warriors stalk the edges of battlefields with cruel patience, their ragged laughter carrying through smoke and tall grass. They favor ambushes, overwhelming isolated foes before fading back into the wilds with whatever spoils they can drag away. A warband’s approach is often marked by scavenged trophies, hyena tracks, and the sudden silence of lesser predators.
