A priest acolyte moves with quiet purpose, carrying incense, sacred symbols, and the steady confidence of practiced ritual. Though not a great champion, they can turn a frightened crowd into a faithful shield or lend courage to companions in desperate moments. Their devotion may be gentle and charitable—or sharpened into zeal when their sanctuary is threatened.
