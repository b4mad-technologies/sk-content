A berserker enters battle like a storm breaking over dry ground, heedless of pain and hungry for the clash of steel. Many bear the scars of hard roads, bitter feuds, or rituals that taught them to turn fury into a weapon. Once roused, their reckless charge can shatter a cautious line before calmer warriors find their footing.
