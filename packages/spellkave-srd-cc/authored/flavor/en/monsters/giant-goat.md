A giant goat is a wall of muscle, horn, and stubborn momentum, equally at home on windswept crags and narrow mountain trails. Its heavy hooves find purchase where most creatures would slip, while its sudden charges can turn a peaceful hillside into a dangerous battlefield. Herds are wary of strangers and fiercely protective of their young.
