A hydra erupts from reeds, caverns, or black water as a tangle of ravenous heads bound to one immense, thrashing body. Each maw snaps with a different hunger, arguing in hisses before striking together. Wounding the beast can make the fight worse, as its terrible vitality answers violence with fresh fangs.
