A cultist wears devotion like a second skin, speaking softly of hidden truths and promised transformations. Alone, they may seem nervous or unremarkable; among fellow believers, ritual certainty hardens into dangerous resolve. Their shrines are often marked by candles, strange symbols, and offerings meant to attract something that should remain distant.
