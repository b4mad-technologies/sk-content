A living cloud of wings, legs, and needle-fine mouths pours from cracks in wood, stone, and soil. Alone, each insect is harmless; together, they strip courage from seasoned travelers as surely as they foul torches, rations, and open wounds. Their restless buzz swells whenever warm blood or sweet scent lingers nearby.
