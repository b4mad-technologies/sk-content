A hill giant’s presence turns a quiet valley into a place of toppled trees, scattered livestock, and panicked flight. Slow to anger but quicker to hunger, these towering raiders descend from high places in search of easy food and anything worth carrying away. Their crude camps sprawl around cookfires, marked by boulders used as tools, toys, and warnings.
