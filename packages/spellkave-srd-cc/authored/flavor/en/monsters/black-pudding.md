A black pudding seeps through ruins and sewers with the patience of a spreading stain, pooling in darkness until movement stirs its hungry awareness. Metal groans and wood softens wherever it passes, leaving corridors scarred by its corrosive touch. Its shapeless bulk can pour through cracks that seem far too small to admit any danger.
