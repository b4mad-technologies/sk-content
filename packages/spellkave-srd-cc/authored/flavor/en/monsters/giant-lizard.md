Giant lizards lurk in sun-baked ruins, steaming marshes, and shadowed caverns, where their stillness is easy to mistake for stone. When prey wanders close, they erupt with startling speed, jaws snapping and tails sweeping aside anything in their path. Some can be driven off with fire and noise; others defend their chosen territory with cold, reptilian persistence.
