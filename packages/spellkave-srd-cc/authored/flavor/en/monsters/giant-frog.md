A giant frog lurks where reeds grow thick and the water hides more than it reveals. Its sudden croak can roll across a marsh like a warning bell, followed by the splash of a powerful leap. Though often content to wait patiently for prey, it becomes fiercely territorial around its favored pool or muddy bank.
