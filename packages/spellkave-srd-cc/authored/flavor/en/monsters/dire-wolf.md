Dire wolves hunt with a grim patience that makes ordinary wolves seem like restless pups. Their heavy paws leave deep tracks through snow and mud, often circling a trail long before the pack closes in. When the howls begin, they carry the promise of coordinated teeth, crushing weight, and no easy escape.
