Warrior infantry form the hard, practical core of any army, advancing behind scarred shields and weathered banners. Their discipline is not born of glory alone, but of long marches, crowded camps, and the grim knowledge that survival depends on the soldier beside them. In battle, they fight as a living wall of steel, stubbornly holding ground others would abandon.
