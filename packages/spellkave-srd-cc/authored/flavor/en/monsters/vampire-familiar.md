A vampire familiar is a small, watchful servant steeped in grave-cold magic, often taking the shape of a bat, rat, cat, or other nocturnal creature. Its eyes gleam with borrowed hunger as it slips through cracks, listens at shutters, and returns to its master with whispered secrets. Even in bright rooms, its shadow seems reluctant to leave the walls.
