A xorn moves through solid stone as easily as a fish through dark water, leaving only a faint tremor and the smell of freshly split mineral. Its stony hide and three-limbed gait make it seem more like a living fault in the earth than a beast. Drawn by veins of precious ore, it may ignore intruders—unless they stand between it and a particularly rich seam.
