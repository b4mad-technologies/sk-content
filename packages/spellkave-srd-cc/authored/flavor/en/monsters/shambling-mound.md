A shambling mound lurches from tangled reeds and rotting brush with the patience of a bog swallowing a stone. Its sodden bulk smells of peat, crushed leaves, and stagnant water, while pale glimmers flicker deep between knotted vines. Travelers often mistake it for harmless driftwood until the vegetation begins to close around them.
