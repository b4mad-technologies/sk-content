An ettin lumbers from the wilds like a quarrel given flesh, its two heads forever snarling, arguing, and occasionally agreeing on where to find the next meal. Each watches a different path, making ambush difficult and escape uncertain. Their crude camps stink of smoke, spoiled meat, and the trophies of travelers who mistook bickering for weakness.
