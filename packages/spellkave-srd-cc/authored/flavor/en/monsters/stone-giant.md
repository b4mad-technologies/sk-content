Stone giants move through high, lonely places with the patience of weathered cliffs. They speak little, but every thrown boulder and booming footfall carries a warning to trespassers. In their hidden halls beneath the mountains, they shape stone into austere monuments and remember insults longer than any mortal dynasty.
